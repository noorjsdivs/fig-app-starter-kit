# The starter pack

The two folders chapter 1 of the [course](https://youtu.be/dRi0hSMB4r8) copies into your new project:

| Folder | What's inside |
| --- | --- |
| `design/` | `fig-and-fennel.dc.html`: the interactive prototype of every screen, with its colours, fonts, icons and data. Keep `support.js` and `uploads/` next to it, then open the `.html` in Chrome, Safari or Firefox. No server is needed |
| `assets/` | `images/icon.png` (the app icon), the three Android adaptive-icon layers (`android-icon-background.png`, `-foreground.png`, `-monochrome.png`), `splash-icon.png` (the splash badge) and `favicon.png` |

**How to use it:**
1. Create the project with `npx create-expo-app@latest fig-and-fennel --template default@sdk-58`, and clean the template.
2. Copy `design/` and `assets/` into the project's root folder, replacing the template's `assets/`.

The [guide](../GUIDE.md#chapter-1-step-by-step) has every step.
